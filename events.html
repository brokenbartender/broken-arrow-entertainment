Pride in Pontiac 2026: Still Here, Still Proud

Pontiac's first-ever Pride event. Produced by Broken Arrow Entertainment in partnership with Unorthodox Drag.

Date

August 14, 2026

Venue

The Crofoot, Pontiac MI

Entry

Free noon–3PM · Ticketed 3PM+

Produced by

Broken Arrow Entertainment & Unorthodox Drag

About the event

Michigan's first Pride event in the city of Pontiac. A full day of drag performances headlined by Stela Vulva, live music and DJ sets, an LGBTQ+-owned vendor marketplace, and community health resources including rapid HIV testing and mental health support. Doors open at 11AM for VIP early access, with free all-ages entry from noon to 3PM before the ticketed evening show begins.

Visit prideinpontiac.com →

Recognized By

The state and city already vouch for us

Pride in Pontiac 2026 was formally recognized by the State of Michigan and the City of Pontiac — a rare, documented endorsement for a first-year grassroots Pride event. Presented jointly to Broken Arrow Entertainment and Unorthodox Drag.
