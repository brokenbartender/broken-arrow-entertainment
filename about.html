About Us

Built in the rooms we grew up working.

Broken Arrow Entertainment LLC is a Pontiac, Michigan-based event production company — run by people who've spent years behind the booth, not behind a desk.

Where we started

Broken Arrow Entertainment grew out of hands-on work in Michigan's bar and nightlife scene: running sound for drag shows, hosting karaoke nights, and helping local venues put on events that actually fill a room. That ground-level experience is the foundation the company is built on — we know what a show needs because we've run one.

Where we're going

In 2026, Broken Arrow Entertainment is producing Pride in Pontiac — Pontiac's first-ever Pride event, in partnership with Unorthodox Drag. It's the biggest production the company has taken on, and the first of what's meant to become an annual tradition for the city.
